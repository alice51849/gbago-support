What this app is

GBAGo is a game emulator for iPhone and iPad. You import your own .gba, .gbc and .gb files (or a .zip containing one) from the Files app and play at 60 fps with touch controls or a game controller. Its focus is save safety: every checkpoint is visible, checked and easy to return to.

  • Recovery Map: one screen per game with the resume point, save state slots, automatic snapshots and the battery save, each marked Ready to load or Not safe to load
  • Safe Continue: resumes from your newest checkpoint only when it checks out; a damaged checkpoint is never loaded silently
  • Visual save slots: 4 to start, expandable to 12, each with a screenshot thumbnail
  • Auto Snapshots about every two minutes during play; the latest 15 stay as thumbnails you can jump back to
  • Auto save when leaving, fast-forward up to 32x, pinch to zoom, screen filters, cheat codes and controller support
  • Game Info: check a file's identity and integrity on device (including SHA-256 and header checksum) and export a JSON report
  • Runs fully on device: no accounts, no ads, no tracking; optional sync through your private iCloud

Who it's for

  • Players who lost hours of progress to a broken or overwritten save in another emulator
  • People who like many save points with screenshots instead of guessing which slot is which
  • Anyone who wants an emulator with no ads and no account
  • People who want to check a file's integrity before playing

FAQ

What is GBAGo?

A game emulator for iPhone and iPad that opens your own .gba, .gbc and .gb files, designed so you never lose progress: Recovery Map, Safe Continue, visual save slots and automatic snapshots.

Does GBAGo include games?

No. Apart from three original Lumi Studio demo cartridges, it contains no games and does not supply or link to game files. Import only files you created yourself or are authorized to use.

How often does it save automatically?

During normal play it takes a quiet snapshot about every two minutes and keeps the latest 15 as thumbnails. It also saves automatically when you leave the app.

How many save slots are there?

4 visual save slots to start, expandable to 12, each with a screenshot thumbnail.

How much does it cost?

It is a paid download with no ads and no account. See the App Store page for the current price in your country.

Can I use a game controller?

Yes, game controllers are supported, along with on-screen touch controls with adjustable size and opacity.

Is there an Android version?

Yes, GBAGo is on the App Store and Google Play.

GBAGo is an independent app and contains no games. Emulation is powered by the open-source mGBA core, licensed under MPL-2.0.
